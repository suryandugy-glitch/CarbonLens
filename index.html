TRANSPARENT CALCULATION

Emission factors & sources

CarbonLens uses activity × emission-factor calculations. The default values below are prototype factors selected for demonstrability; replace them with institution-specific factors when available. Electricity uses India's Central Electricity Authority grid database. Transport factors are adapted from the UK Government 2026 conversion-factor framework; food factors use global food-system research processed by Our World in Data. Waste defaults are conservative prototype values and should be replaced with local waste-model factors for formal reporting.
